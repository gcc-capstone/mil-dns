## Overview

As you begin development, what you build over the next few weeks will not be a complete, shippable product, so it does not quite fit the idea of a Scrum "sprint." Instead, we will call our development target for this semester **Milestone 1**.

For Milestone 1, your goal is to demonstrate that the major components of your application work together: **frontend, backend, and database (or external data source, where appropriate).**

The frontend does not need to be polished or fully styled, but the functionality must work. Focus on getting a small, meaningful portion of your application working from end to end.

For example, you might implement:

- A screen where users can create one of the central entities in your system (a book listing, club, or marketing campaign).
- A screen that retrieves and displays a list of those entities.

Two such create-and-view workflows are typically a reasonable Milestone 1 target. However, different projects may require different functionality. **Discuss your proposed target with your advisor** to determine what is appropriate for your project.

### 2. Individual Responsibilities

**Example:**

Your responsibilities should be specific, realistic, and collectively sufficient to complete the milestone.
